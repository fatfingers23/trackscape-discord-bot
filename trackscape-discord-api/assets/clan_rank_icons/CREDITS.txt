Clan rank icons

These icons are Old School RuneScape interface graphics. They are the property
of Jagex Ltd. and are used here to show a clan mate's rank next to their name.
TrackScape is not affiliated with or endorsed by Jagex.

They were sourced from the Old School RuneScape Wiki, with thanks to its
contributors:
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Clan_Settings#Rank_Titles
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Category:Clan_rank_icons

The originals are 13x13. The copies here were scaled up with nearest-neighbour
by scripts/fetch_clan_rank_icons.py and are otherwise unmodified.
